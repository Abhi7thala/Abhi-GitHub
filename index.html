About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Jyväskylä, Finland, working with Kai Rajala. I received my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar in December 2024, under the supervision of Vasudeva Rao Allu.

My research lies in geometric analysis, with an emphasis on quasiconformal analysis and geometry, and analysis and geometry on metric spaces.

PublicationsResearchGateORCID

Research

  • Quasihyperbolic geometry

    Gromov hyperbolicity, curvature, boundary geometry, and visibility of geodesics.

  • Quasiconformal analysis

    Quasiconformal deformations, weighted Bergman spaces, and quasiregular mappings.

  • Analysis on metric spaces

    Geometric analysis beyond the Euclidean setting, including non-Loewner spaces and uniformization.

  • Complex analysis

    Univalent function theory, coefficient problems, and the Zalcman conjecture.
