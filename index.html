About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Jyväskylä, Finland, working with Kai Rajala. I received my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar in December 2024, under the supervision of Vasudeva Rao Allu.

My research lies in geometric analysis, with an emphasis on quasiconformal analysis and geometry, and analysis and geometry on metric spaces.

Research

My primary research interests lie in geometric mapping theory and the analysis and geometry of metric spaces.

  • Quasihyperbolic geometry

    Geometric and curvature properties of the quasihyperbolic metric, including Gromov hyperbolicity, Alexandrov geometry, and visibility; connections with the hyperbolic metric on planar domains.

  • Conformal and quasiconformal geometry

    Quasiconformal deformations of abstract uniform domains; conformal and quasiconformal uniformization of metric surfaces, fractal spaces, and multiply connected planar domains, including questions related to Koebe’s circle domain conjecture. Quasiconformal homogeneity of hyperbolic Riemann surfaces.

  • Hardy and Bergman theory for geometric mappings

    Hardy, weighted Hardy, and weighted Bergman theory for quasiconformal and quasiregular mappings.

  • Transboundary modulus and duality

    Duality properties of transboundary modulus and their connections with conformal uniformization.

  • Complex analysis and geometry

    Univalent function theory and coefficient problems.
